- **Operators of stores that learned an estimator calibration under context-manager 0.13 or earlier:** context-manager 0.14.0 stamps its persisted calibration multiplier with the pricing it was learned under. An unstamped multiplier is kept and re-stamped on upgrade, unless the store holds XML-history carriers, in which case it restarts from 1.0. Two kinds of store can then refuse every compile with `OverBudgetError` before inference: one whose multiplier was last learned before context-manager 0.11.0, and one that holds XML-history carriers and had learned a multiplier below 1. **Migration:** with the host stopped, reset the first kind's multiplier once, and re-stamp the second kind's checked value, as the **Operators** entry of context-manager 0.14.0's changelog describes. **Unchanged:** a store without XML-history carriers whose multiplier was learned on context-manager 0.11.0 or later keeps that multiplier, re-stamped.
