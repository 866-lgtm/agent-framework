- `WorkspaceModule.readFileFromDisk` and `read_image` no longer wait on a FIFO in a mount until something writes to it. They refuse a FIFO, socket or device without opening it, `readFileFromDisk` as `not_found` with "Not a regular file: <path>", which is also its answer now for a file that isn't regular behind a followed symlink, where it said "File not found".
