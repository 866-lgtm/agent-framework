- A turn woken only by deferred coalescing batches is withdrawn before inference in three more cases where it used to call the model with nothing new to read: its batch was retracted or replaced after the wake was taken, its batch was rendering for another agent's assembly (the turn now waits for that render and reads it), or the render was placed in another agent's deferred queue because that agent's turn was running. In the last case that agent is now woken for the render; before, nothing woke it for it once its turn ended. When several agents are woken for one batch, the first assembly consumes it, and the others' turns already taken from the queue are withdrawn, as their still-queued wakes already were.
