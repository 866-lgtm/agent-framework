- A context budget the agent was running at comes back at once after a restart, when it still fits (context-manager #162). Before, every restore of a lower budget paced a new descent from the recipe's budget, so until the first compile settled it the agent reported `converging` and planned at the recipe's budget.
  - **The marker.** The persisted runtime settings carry `contextBudgetReachedTokens` beside `contextBudgetTokens`: the budget the agent's last compile planned at, written when a compile fits at the record's budget (a paced descent's settle included) and withdrawn when the context manager refuses that budget as over budget, even if the budget changed while that compile ran. A fit counts only if the live budget didn't change while the compile ran, and a settle only for a compile that began under that descent; each arm of a descent is a new one, to the same target included. What a compile showed stands only until the live budget changes: after a persisted change, immediate or paced, back to an earlier budget included, the record carries none until a compile fits at the new budget. A `persist: false` change leaves the record as it was, marker included.
  - **At boot**, a record whose two values match is applied at once, but only if the preflight says the budget fits now. The preflight plans the stored context as the context manager will, so it sees a new `maxTokens` or strategy configuration a restart brings, and a context that grew while the agent ran at another budget; the system prompt and tools are outside the context manager's budget. If the first compile at a budget applied at once below the recipe's is refused, the agent restores it paced from the recipe's budget and compiles again, or, if it can't be restored paced, compiles at the recipe's budget with the budget kept set. A tail, pace or think-text policy change after the boot leaves that fallback in place; a change of the budget or the descent ends it.
  - **Otherwise** each persisted setting is restored on its own, the think-text policy first and the budget last, a lower budget paced as before. That covers a record without the field (a descent still running at the stop, or a record from an earlier release) and one with a value for another budget.
  - **A persisted setting never stops the agent booting,** except an invalid think-text policy, which still fails closed. A setting that can't be applied now (a budget no longer above `maxTokens`, a lower one on a strategy that can't prepare a smaller window, a tail or pace on one without live settings) is kept set but unapplied, so a later persisting write keeps it in the record, and the agent runs at the recipe's for it. A `private` think-text policy beside such a budget still governs. One log line says why the budget wasn't applied at once, what couldn't be applied, and the budget that's set beside the one compiles plan at.
  - **Smaller:** a patch can't set the field; an invalid value is ignored with a log line naming its kind; a record holding nothing else restores nothing. A record the boot can't read is logged, and the agent boots at the recipe's. A record that can't be read or written when a compile tells it is logged, and told again at the next compile.
- `previewAgentRuntimeSettings` and the budget preflight plan a patch at the reach its next compile plans at: a descent's pace while a window is prepared for it (one the patch arms, or one in flight that it leaves alone), and the configured reach otherwise, so an immediate change, and a pace with no descent, preview at the configured reach. A preview taken during a descent still plans under that descent's prepared window, and at its live pace when the strategy carries one.
- The runtime settings snapshot (`getAgentRuntimeSettings`, `agent_settings` `get`) reports `effectiveContextBudgetTokens`, the budget compiles plan at now, beside `contextBudgetTokens`, the budget that's set. They differ while a lower budget converges, when compiles still plan at the budget the descent started from, and while a budget the agent couldn't apply is kept, when they plan at the budget it runs at. Code that builds an `AgentRuntimeSettingsSnapshot` itself, such as a test double, adds the field.
