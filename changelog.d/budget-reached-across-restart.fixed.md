- A lower context budget an agent had reached comes back reached after a restart (context-manager #162). The persisted runtime settings now record `contextBudgetReached: true` beside `contextBudgetTokens` once the live budget is at it: when an immediate decrease applies, or when a paced descent settles, which until now wrote nothing. A restore with the marker applies the budget at once, through the immediate path. Before, every restore paced a new descent from the recipe's budget, so until the first compile settled it the agent reported `converging` and planned at the recipe's budget. Since the store fit that budget when it was reached, the planner can fold to it at once even if its carried frontier was lost. A record without the marker restores paced, as before: a descent still running at the stop, or a record from an earlier release. A descent applied with `persist: false` writes nothing when it settles, and an invalid marker is ignored with a log line.
- The runtime settings snapshot (`getAgentRuntimeSettings`, `agent_settings` `get`) reports `effectiveContextBudgetTokens`, the budget compiles plan at now. While a lower budget converges, it is still the budget the descent started from; `contextBudgetTokens` stays the budget the agent is set to.
