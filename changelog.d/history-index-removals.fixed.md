- The history module's semantic index no longer stops taking removals because one came before the index was made. The removals and edits phase runs before any upsert, so a removal made before a store's first pass went to `/delete` before its index existed. When `/delete` answered 404, as a service may for a namespace not made yet, every later removal was dropped until the host restarted or bound the module again, and removed messages stayed in the index, which only grew; search-time checks keep them out of results. A pass whose `/stats` answers 404 now has no index to remove from: it drops its removals, and the deletes of messages edited down to nothing indexable, without calling `/delete`. None can come into the index that pass makes, since the time walk upserts only what the store still holds and can index. A 404 from `/delete` after `/stats` has answered still means a service without that route, and still ends removals until the host restarts.
