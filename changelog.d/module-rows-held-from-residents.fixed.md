- A module's `ctx.queryMessages` and `ctx.getMessage` now mark a message a tune-out holds from the residents with `heldFromResidents: true` (on `ModuleMessage`). Such a message arrived in a tuned-out channel and is stored with `metadata.tuneOut`, and no resident's compiled view shows it, during the tune-out or after it. A cancel brings the residents the text of the newest stored ones, up to the tune-out's `backlogCap`, in one `<tuned-out-backlog>` row of its own, which isn't marked. A module's context answered it as an ordinary row, so a module that leaves out `held: true` rows to keep to what the residents see took it in. A held write that carries the stamp is marked as well as `held`, and so is its stored row once it lands.
