- `getActiveTurnTrigger` returns the trigger of a turn that a coalesced batch woke without the host-owned `coalescingSubject`, `coalescingBatch` and `coalescingBatchSubjects` while that turn is retried: an in-turn retry, a provider hold's release, a requeue behind an auxiliary call, or the tool-result guard's restart. Those fields identify a wake that hasn't been consumed, for withdrawal, and such a turn has consumed its batches. Its other fields, such as `reason`, `source`, `channelId` and `counterparty`, are as before.
