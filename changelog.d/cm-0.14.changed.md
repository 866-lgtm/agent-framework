- Depend on `@animalabs/context-manager` ^0.14.0. A preview, or any compile made with `dryRun`, now leaves the context manager's estimate, its calibration and a pending kv-unified draft's marker indices as it found them. The context manager's state queries (`isReady`, `getPendingWork`, `getHotContextSettings`, `previewContext` and others) now throw while its strategy's initialization is pending or has failed, where they used to answer. A reposted sharded document now renders as two bodies, so a stored conversation holding one misses the prompt cache once after the upgrade.
  - **Operators:** context-manager 0.14.0 stamps its persisted calibration multiplier with the pricing it was learned under. Two kinds of store can refuse every compile with `OverBudgetError` after the upgrade: one whose multiplier was last learned before context-manager 0.11.0, and one that holds XML-history carriers and had learned a multiplier below 1. Each needs its multiplier reset or re-stamped once, as the "Operators" entry in context-manager 0.14.0's changelog describes.
