- The inference-failure pipeline bounds the failure text it records. A failure's text is its source's `Error.message`, and a provider can echo the whole rejected request there: in one household an OpenAI-compatible 400 echoed ~1.7 MB, and the framework stored it in the resident's own context as an `[inference-failed]` marker (about 200K tokens, by the household's notes, which the resident then carried), wrote it to stderr and `logs/failures.log`, and traced it.
  - Every cut of failure text follows one rule: the text's head and tail around a marker stating how many of how many characters were omitted. Each sink cuts the whole text, so its marker states the whole length, and no cut separates a surrogate pair.
  - The `[inference-failed]` marker carries a 600-character excerpt. It also names the classification when there is one ("the model call failed (invalid_request, HTTP 400) …"). Its metadata adds `errorType`, `retryable`, `httpStatus` and `providerErrorCode`, plus `reasonChars` whenever the marker's excerpt was cut. A small unclassified failure produces exactly the marker it did before. Markers already stored keep their size until they're folded or removed.
  - The rewind marker keeps 160 characters of the reason; an agent's `lastInference.lastError` and the compile-refused ops alert keep 300; the ops webhook posts 500 characters of the alert's message.
  - These sinks carry at most 2,000 characters:
    - the reason excerpt in the exhaustion stderr line (the line also carries the agent, the streak and `type=… status=… code=…` labels when known);
    - every field of a `logs/failures.log` record: a string to 2,000 characters, an `Error` as its projection (below), and any other value as its JSON, kept as data when it fits and as bounded text when it doesn't. A record with a value JSON can't write is no longer lost;
    - ops alert messages, and an alert's `data` on its `ops:alert` trace, bounded field by field as a failures.log record is;
    - the inference log's `error`;
    - every trace event's `error` and `stack` strings;
    - the provider cooldown's reason, in its stderr lines and the status snapshot's `cooldownReason`;
    - the error an ephemeral run (`runEphemeralToCompletion`) rejects with.

    `inference:exhausted` traces and failures.log records add `httpStatus` and `providerErrorCode`, and a failures.log record adds `reasonChars` when its own excerpt was cut. The error type and the provider code are recorded to at most 128 characters each.
  - The OverBudget drain kick, the marker write and the provider-cooldown resume used to print the error object when they failed. They now print a bounded projection instead: name, classification, status, provider code and message, each part bounded and the whole within 2,000 characters, on one line unless one of its parts has newlines, as a message can. The stack's frames follow on the lines below, also bounded to 2,000 characters. Printing a `MembraneError` prints its `rawRequest`, so a long system prompt was written out in full. Context maintenance records and logs a bounded message, in its existing format.
  - The OverBudget drain breaker's message-text fallback now applies only to unclassified failures, and it is decided on the whole reason, never the excerpt. A classified provider error that quotes "exceed hard budget" or "no summary covers" (the agent's own context, echoed back) no longer kicks the compression drain.
- This bound doesn't depend on membrane bounding its own errors.
- A `MembraneError` from another copy of membrane is recognised by its name as well as by its class, since the host supplies the `Membrane` instance and two copies can be installed. Its failure is classified, the default error policy reads its `retryable` and `retryAfterMs`, and an organization acceleration limit it reports is held, as for the framework's own copy. Before, each treated it as an unknown error: the default policy retried a non-retryable one up to three times, and its failure was then left unclassified.
- Not covered by this change: Discord-awareness log sites, the speech-route failure reason and `[discord-send-failed]` marker (kept whole as the delivery evidence a resident reads before resending), and other module or hook logs that print error objects.
